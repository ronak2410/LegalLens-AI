Plain-Language Legal Terms Glossary

Demystifying dense contractual terms so non-lawyers can evaluate risks with confidence.

Indemnification (Hold Harmless)

An agreement where you promise to pay the other party's legal defense costs and court damages if someone sues them because of your work. Watch out for uncapped or one-sided indemnities.

Liquidated Damages

A pre-agreed fixed dollar penalty you must pay if you break the contract early, regardless of whether the other party suffered real financial losses.

Severability Clause

Ensures that if a court rules one specific clause illegal or unenforceable, the rest of the contract remains active and binding.

Governing Law & Forum Selection

Decides which state's laws control the contract and which city/court you must travel to if a lawsuit occurs.

Force Majeure

Excuses parties from performance obligations during unforeseeable catastrophic events (natural disasters, pandemics, government wars).

Work Made for Hire

A copyright doctrine meaning the client automatically owns everything you create from day one, including preliminary sketches or code.
